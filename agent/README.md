# Footnote: new-hire jargon decoder

Footnote helps a new Halcyon Robotics teammate understand company terms during onboarding. Ask it what a project or acronym means and it answers from the company glossary with the responsible person and team. For terms learned in recent meetings, it retrieves the saved meaning and labels inferred contacts as suggestions. Give it a meeting title or agenda: it reads the glossary and recent meeting digest, uses StepFun to select up to five relevant terms, and proposes three questions with contacts. Definitions and contacts come from saved records; a keyword brief is available if StepFun fails. It can also recap terms learned and suggest people to meet. Lookups and prep do not record sightings. Ask to review saved questions and the agent shows the latest ended meeting's pending drafts. Each email requires an explicit, expiring approval code in the same conversation; cancellation leaves the draft unsent. This demo sends only to the configured demo inbox, with coworker names used as display recipients.

Keywords: onboarding, company glossary, jargon, acronyms, personalized meeting prep, meeting recap, people to meet, Halcyon Robotics.

Example questions:

- What does Bluebird mean at Halcyon?
- Prep me for my 2pm platform sync
- Prepare me for a telemetry review about Ridgeline and FCS
- Prep me for an incident review about SLO and runbook
- What did I learn today?
- Who should I meet?

- Show the questions I saved in my last meeting
- Send my questions (shows drafts for review; requires a separate approval)
